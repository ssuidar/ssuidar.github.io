Make a copy before every patch. Even though the utility does this automatically, it is worth opening it once before a major update just to confirm a fresh dated snapshot has appeared.

Give your backups room to grow. Each dated copy takes up a bit of disk space. A folder with a few hundred megabytes of free room is plenty for most people.

Keep the copies on a separate drive if you can. A second internal drive or an external USB drive is a good place for the backup folder, because it keeps your snapshots safe if your main drive ever has trouble.

Clean up occasionally. Old snapshots can be deleted by hand once you are confident you will never need them. Delete the oldest ones first and keep the most recent handful.

Do not rename the dated folders. The dates in the folder names are what make the timeline easy to read. Renaming them makes it harder to tell which copy came before a patch.
